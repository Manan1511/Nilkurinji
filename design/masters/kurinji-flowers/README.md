# Nilkurinji flower correction masters

These PNGs are the generated source masters for the blue-violet flower pass. Public WebP/PNG files are storefront derivatives; the original references remain available in Git history where they were tracked.

`product-preview/` contains the generic tea-scene previews that show the Nilkurinji brand flower. Jasmine and hibiscus ingredient scenes are deliberately excluded so those flowers retain their own species colors.

The masters are illustrative. They are not verified botanical plates and do not validate product packaging, sourcing or ingredients. The approved color direction is pale blue through mauve/blue-violet, consistent with [Kew Plants of the World Online](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A55761-1/general-information) and [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/destination/neelakurinji-the-blue-beauty-in-munnar/377//).
