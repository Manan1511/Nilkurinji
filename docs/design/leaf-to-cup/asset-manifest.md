The hero uses the same source image at every viewport size. CSS preserves a reserved 3:2 frame and crops it responsively. No separate mobile crop has been generated locally. Shopify homepage content can provide optional chapter-specific mobile references (`leaf_mobile_image`, `water_mobile_image`, and `cup_mobile_image`) when approved assets are available. The format panels use the 4:5 sources at both desktop and mobile widths.

No Kurinji botanical image is included. The story stays text-led until the owner supplies or approves a verifiable botanical reference. Do not use generated blossoms as evidence of botanical accuracy.

## Replacement checklist

- Replace illustrative hero and format scenes with approved, accurately coloured photography.
- Confirm that any product shown is the correct SKU and packaging before it appears in a product gallery.
- Supply mobile crops and focal points where a desktop crop would hide the subject.
- Provide a verified Kurinji reference before publishing a botanical illustration or calling artwork botanically accurate.
- Review alt text against the final image and the actual product data.
