## Composition

- The homepage hero pairs copy on the left with one landscape tea photograph on the right.
- A separate two-column format section below the hero uses the transparent tea and tea-bag cutouts on parchment.
- One compact brand note uses the existing transparent leaf artwork beside the copy. Keep it fully visible and do not repeat it as page-edge framing.
- Neither cutout is clipped, masked into a circle, or cropped into its own oval tile. Keep enough negative space for each silhouette to remain distinct.
- On narrow screens, stack hero copy above the photo. Keep format imagery contained in its card and labels outside the alpha image bounds.
- Product photography remains colour-accurate and follows the shared product-card aspect ratio.

## Accuracy and delivery

The two alpha cutouts and preview product images are illustrative. They do not establish ingredients, origin, harvest, grade, bag construction, package design, or product specifications. Live product imagery must come from approved Shopify media. Live routes must never fall back to preview imagery after a Shopify error.

Check alpha preservation, intrinsic dimensions, alternate text, responsive crop, and loading behaviour after replacing or adding an image. Do not embed labels or claims inside the image file.
