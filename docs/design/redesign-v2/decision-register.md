# Nilkurinji redesign direction

The owner requested implementation of the detailed plan on 20 October 2026. Execute its recommended editorial tea shop direction in the previously chosen native workflow.

## Direction contract

Preserve the existing logo artwork/lettering and forest green. Replace the presentation with Newsreader editorial headings, Manrope controls/body, near-white grounds and open product grids. The first viewport uses one compact 76px header (64px compact), a short preview notice, a framed product/tea image beside a concise headline and one Tea action. Mobile puts copy/action before media.

The signature interaction is search without page displacement: a bounded desktop popover and a compact modal sheet. Motion provides short feedback and one restrained hero entrance. No repeated foliage, ornamental gold, giant masthead, circular category shelf or inactive preview purchase control survives.

Shop, collections, results and recommendations share cards and alignment. Product/Cart/Wishlist emphasize reliable selection, totals and recovery. Reading pages share the shell and controlled text measure. Exact values are in storefront-tokens.css after implementation; no screenshot evidence is implied by this contract.

## Review boundary

The request authorizes implementation without another method/concept question. Browser automation remains excluded. Owner-provided rendered desktop/mobile review is still required before visual sign-off; build/DOM tests prove a narrower scope.
