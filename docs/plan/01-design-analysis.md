# 01. Design analysis

## Measurement

Both PDFs were rasterised at 144 DPI and their embedded screenshots inspected at native 1536 × 1024. Allow 2–4px edge uncertainty due to compression. Values below describe the screenshot, not an original design file.

## B2 final homepage

| Section | Approximate y position | Required composition |
|---|---:|---|
| Header | 0–123 | Cream, two rows, search left, centred stacked logo, utilities right, centred navigation below |
| Hero | 123–374 | Shallow 251px photo banner, pale text field left, tea pack/cup/landscape right |

Use a consistent 124px desktop header. Match logo allocation (about 244px including descriptor), search width (about 187px), and three utility positions. Use equal product tracks within the approximately 1312px featured area. Product media is landscape, near 16:9. Trust-bar claims require business approval. Append the A3 footer; the trust band is not the footer.

## A2 Shop base and A1 Tea Bags

A2 has a 117px header, 253px banner, category row through y=452, then a left filter sidebar and four product columns. Sidebar starts near x=70, its divider near x=290, and the grid near x=320; card gaps are about 24–28px. Use equal card widths. A1 has a taller banner and three large cards; retain its tea-bag art direction but normalise it to A2’s shared Shop grid.

Shop tabs become All, Tea, Tea Bags, Rice, Snacks and Other Foods. Tea Type, Flavour and Caffeine belong in relevant filters rather than separate category pages. Food categories hide tea-specific filters. Add count, sort, selected-filter chips and pagination without shrinking readable text.

## A3 Tea Guide and footer

A3 uses a shallow photographic banner, sprout-and-rule heading, topic pills, then five editorial cards with images near 8:5. Topics/cards are visually compact; give controls 44px hit areas. Tea Guide uses this page. Recipes and Journal use the same listing/article templates backed by separate Shopify blogs.

The footer shows an inverse logo, newsletter space, social links, brand line, separators and botanical line art. Newsletter is deferred per prior scope; use a working contact block pending client approval. Add legal, shipping, food-licence and grievance details only when supplied.

## A4 Our Story

Sections: shallow hero, origin copy and paper/photo collage, four values, closing banner, global footer. Build collage as separate paper, photo, tape and stamp layers. Do not bake text into images. The reference’s “Since 2020”, Nilgiris origin and other provenance claims are unverified; omit until approved.

## Reconciliation register

Use B2’s shared header and active route state everywhere; merge tea formats into Shop; unify product-card widths and filter vocabulary; retain the A3 editorial visual system; link Our Story from footer/contextual links; publish no unsupported health, origin, quality or delivery claims. Real pack photography replaces generated packaging. Larger text and touch targets are acceptable fidelity adaptations.
